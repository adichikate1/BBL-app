Reality check

“Loss ÷ matches played” does not win your money back: a single win at normal odds pays back only a fraction of the loss, while a few more losses make the stakes bigger. The prediction model is right about 58% of the time on past seasons, and bookmaker margins usually cancel that edge. Treat this as a log that shows the truth, set a stop-loss, and only use money you can afford to lose. Online betting is restricted or illegal in many places in India, so check the rules where you live.
